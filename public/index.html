About The Garamut

A weekly reflection written by Ps Barry for Christian leaders in developing nations, covering prayer, proclamation and personal progress.

A garamut is a traditional Papua New Guinean signal drum, hand-carved from a single log — used for centuries to carry messages across great distances.

About Ps Barry Silverback

Barry Silverback served for many years as a missionary in Papua New Guinea, where he developed a deep love for the people and cultures of the Pacific. He went on to become International Director of CRC Churches International, a role through which he continues to equip and encourage Christian leaders across the developing world.

The Garamut reflections grew from a simple desire to keep serving — to send a word of encouragement, teaching and challenge to leaders who might otherwise feel isolated in their work. Like the garamut drum itself, they carry a message across great distances.

Barry writes each reflection personally, drawing on decades of missionary experience, pastoral wisdom, and a lifelong engagement with Scripture.
